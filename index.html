A LETTER, JUST FOR YOU♡

Dear Jeslyn,

Happy birthday ;3

I was trying to figure out how to start this without sounding too sentimental, but here we are, writing you a whole birthday letter wkwkwk.

I still think about 3 October, the last time we talked. You probably didn’t realise how happy I was that day. In my head, I was just like, “WYM I finally get to lepak dengan you???” 😭 I don’t know what that day was like for you, but I went home happy that I got to spend a little time with you. It’s a memory I’m glad I have.

There are a few things I wish I’d said while you were here. So I guess I’ll say them now.

Jeslyn, I wish you could see yourself through my eyes sometimes. Especially on the days when you’re being hard on yourself, or wondering whether you’re enough. You’re such a beautiful woman, and there’s so much about you to admire beyond that. The way you think, the strength you’ve had to find, the person you’re still becoming. I hope you learn to appreciate those parts of yourself too.

I know this year has been hell for you. I won’t pretend I understand everything you’ve felt, but from what you’ve shared with me, I know it hasn’t been easy. And I’m so, so proud of you. I don’t think I’ve told you that enough.

I hope you know you don’t have to be strong every day for that to still be true.

You can be tired. You can have days when you don’t know what you’re doing. I’m still proud of you, Jeslyn.

I’m sorry I couldn’t be there on the day of your flight. I wish I’d had the chance to see you, say goodbye properly, and tell you to take care of yourself in person. I didn’t really know what I wanted to say then. Honestly, I’m still figuring it out as I write this.

But I want you to know that whatever I’ve done for you, I did because I wanted to. I wanted to spend time with you. I wanted to show up for you. I never had to convince myself to care.

Maybe I like you more than I know how to admit. Idk man, you make it very difficult to act normal wkwkwk.

I don’t really have a neat explanation for my feelings yet. What I do know is that I’m happy I got to know you, and I cherish the time we’ve had together. Even now, when we don’t talk as much, I still find myself wondering how you’re doing and hoping life is treating you well.

So, for your birthday, I hope this next year gives you something to look forward to. I hope you get to laugh without having so much on your mind. I hope you meet people you feel comfortable being yourself around. And I hope, little by little, you start believing the good things people say about you.

Because I mean every word I’ve written here, even if I have to hide behind a few “wkwkwk”s to say it.

Happy birthday, Jeslyn. I wish I could say all this to you in person, but until then, this little corner of the internet is yours.

Jaga diri baik-baik over there, okay? There’s someone here who’s really glad he got to know you.

Me lah, obviously ;3
Haffizol ♡
