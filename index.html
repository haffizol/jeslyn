Dear Jeslyn,

Happy birthday ;3

Idk how to start this without sounding all sentimental, so I’m just going to say what I wanted to say. Bear with me okay wkwkwk.

I still think about 3 October. My god, I was so happy that day. WYM I finally got to lepak dengan you??? 😭 You probably didn’t know how much it meant to me, but I went home happy just because I got to spend that time with you. I still smile when I think about it.

It’s been a while since we last talked, and there are a few things I never really got to tell you.

First of all, you’re beautiful lah. I know me saying that won’t magically make all your insecurities disappear, but I do wish you could see what I see sometimes. There’s so much I like about you. The way you think, the way you carry yourself. Even the little things you probably don’t notice about yourself.

And I’m proud of you, Jeslyn. So, so proud. This year has been hell for you, and I know I only know the parts you’ve told me. I can’t pretend I understand all of it. But I know it took a lot to get through those days.

You don’t have to be okay every day. I’m still proud of you.

I hope you let yourself rest sometimes. Cry if you need to. Be tired. You don’t always have to know what to do next, okay?

I’m sorry I couldn’t be there on the day of your flight. I wish I’d seen you off properly. I think I would’ve struggled to say all this anyway, but at least I could’ve told you to jaga diri in person.

There’s also something I’ve been trying to explain to myself. Everything I did for you, I did because I wanted to. Nobody had to ask me. I just wanted to spend time with you, do little things for you, and be there when I could.

Maybe I just like you lah. Idk man. Weird ah feelings wkwkwk.

I’m still figuring that part out. But I know I’m glad I got to know you. I cherish the time we’ve had, even if I didn’t say much about it then. And even though we don’t talk as often now, I still wonder how you’re doing. I hope you’re eating properly, getting enough rest, and finding things that make you happy over there.

For your birthday, I really hope this next year is kinder to you. I hope there are more good days. More laughing until your stomach hurts. More days where you wake up and actually look forward to something. And I hope you start being a little kinder to yourself too.

You deserve good things, Jeslyn. I mean that.

I know I’ve put a lot of wkwkwk in here, but that’s because I get shy lah 😭 I do mean everything I’ve written.

So happy birthday, again. This is my little way of celebrating you from here. I hope you like it, even if it’s a bit much for a birthday wish hehe.

Jaga diri baik-baik, okay? There’s someone here who’s really glad you came into his life.

Me lah, obviously ;3
